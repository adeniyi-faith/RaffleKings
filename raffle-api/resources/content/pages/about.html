RaffleKings is an online raffle platform where you can win cash, gadgets and other prizes for the price of a ticket. It is run by RKS DIGITAL INNOVATIONS, a business registered in Nigeria.

How it works

  1. Create a free account. You must be 18 or older.
  2. Top up your wallet securely with Paystack, or by bank transfer.
  3. Pick a raffle and your lucky numbers. Each raffle shows its prizes, ticket price, how many tickets there are and when it closes. Buying more tickets at once can get you a discount.
  4. Watch the draw. When the raffle closes, the winners are drawn. Some draws are shown live, and you can chat and react while you watch.
  5. Get paid. Cash prizes go straight into your Winnings, which you can withdraw to your bank account. We contact winners of other prizes to arrange delivery.

Fair, and you can check it

Every draw is provably fair. Before a draw, we lock in a secret random code and publish its fingerprint, so it can't be changed afterwards. After the draw, anyone can open the raffle's "Verify this draw" page and check that the winners came from that code and the tickets sold. Nobody, including us, can pick the winners.

More ways to win

  • Daily rewards: collect points every day. The longer your streak, the bigger the reward.
  • Spin & Win: use your points to spin the wheel. The real odds are always shown.
  • Refer & Earn: earn commission when friends you invite top up for the first time.

Your money is safe

Payments are handled by licensed payment partners, and we never see your card details. Every naira in and out of your account is recorded, and you can see your full history under Transactions.

Play responsibly

RaffleKings is for fun. Only spend what you can afford, and take a break whenever you need one. Our support team is always happy to help.
