=== start-here-welcome-tour
category: Getting started
title: Start here: a quick tour of RaffleKings
excerpt: What RaffleKings is, how a raffle works, and where everything lives, in five minutes.
time: 5 min
featured: yes
image: home-top
caption: The home screen shows live raffles, your balance and the bottom menu.

RaffleKings lets you buy numbered tickets for a raffle. When every ticket is sold, or the closing time arrives, one fair draw picks the winners. The prize can be cash, a gadget or a voucher. Here is the whole journey in simple steps.

1. **Create a free account.** You get a {{welcome_bonus}} welcome bonus in your Spending Wallet. See [How to create your account](/support/tutorials/create-your-account).
2. **Add money to your wallet** with the "Top Up Wallet" page. Pay with card, bank transfer or USSD on Paystack's secure page.
3. **Choose a raffle** from the Raffles tab. Open one you like and tap "Get tickets".
4. **Pick how many tickets** and then pick your lucky numbers.
5. **Pay at checkout** from your wallet (or from your winnings). You are given a receipt and your numbers show under My Tickets.
6. **Wait for the draw.** You can watch it live or check the result later in the Hall of Fame.
7. **If you win,** cash prizes land in your Winnings balance. You can withdraw Winnings to your bank account.

Good to know:
- Your **Spending Wallet** buys tickets only. Your **Winnings** can be withdrawn or moved to the wallet.
- The bottom bar has five tabs: Home, Raffles, Winners, My Rewards and Profile.
- Every draw can be checked by anyone on its "Verify" page, so it is fair and open.

=== what-is-a-raffle
category: Getting started
title: What is a raffle and how does the draw work?
excerpt: A plain-English explanation of tickets, numbers, prizes and how winners are chosen.
time: 3 min

A raffle is a draw where each ticket gives you one chance to win. Each ticket carries a number. The more tickets you hold in a raffle, the better your chance of winning in that raffle.

1. Open any raffle and look at the prize, the price per ticket and the progress bar. The bar shows how many tickets are sold and how many are left.
2. Buy one or more tickets and pick your numbers.
3. When the raffle closes, a draw picks the winning ticket numbers at random.
4. Winners are announced in the Hall of Fame, in your Notifications, and by email.

Good to know:
- A raffle closes when all tickets are sold or when its closing date arrives, whichever comes first. Sales close at the end of that day, Nigerian time.
- Each person can win once in the same raffle by default.
- Every draw is "provably fair". Anyone can check it on the raffle's Verify page.

=== how-winners-are-chosen
category: Getting started
title: How RaffleKings picks winners fairly
excerpt: The draw is random, locked in before it runs, and anyone can check it.
time: 3 min

Nobody at RaffleKings can pick a winner by hand. The draw uses a random method that is locked in before the draw runs, and you can check it yourself.

1. **Before sales close**, a secret code is made and only its "fingerprint" is published. This proves the code was not changed later.
2. **When the draw runs**, the secret code is mixed with a code built from the real list of tickets.
3. **After the draw**, the secret code is shown. You can see that it matches the fingerprint.
4. Open the raffle's **Verify** page (from My Tickets or the Hall of Fame) to see four checks, each marked as passed.

Good to know:
- You do not need technical knowledge. If all four checks show as passed, the draw was fair.
- See also [How to verify a draw](/support/tutorials/verify-a-draw-step-by-step).

=== create-your-account
category: Getting started
title: How to create your account
excerpt: Sign up in under a minute and receive your welcome bonus.
time: 2 min
image: signup-form
caption: The sign-up form asks for a username, email and password.

Creating an account is free and takes under a minute. You receive a {{welcome_bonus}} welcome bonus in your Spending Wallet.

1. Tap **Profile** in the bottom bar, then **Create Free Account** (or open the Register page).
2. Choose a **username** of 3 to 60 characters. Use letters, numbers, underscore, dot or dash.
3. Type your **email address**. You will use it to log in.
4. Choose a **password** of at least 8 characters with at least one letter and one number.
5. If you have a promo code, tap "Have a promo code?" and type it. This box only shows when promo codes are switched on.
6. Tick the box that says you are 18 or older and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
7. Tap **Create account**. You are signed in straight away.

Good to know:
- There is no email confirmation step. You can start right away.
- Your username is also your referral code, so choose one you are happy to share.
- You must be 18 or older to play.

Stuck: If you see "That username is already taken", try a different one. See [Sign-up errors](/support/tutorials/sign-up-errors-explained).

=== sign-up-errors-explained
category: Getting started
title: Sign-up errors explained and how to fix them
excerpt: What each message on the sign-up form means and what to change.
time: 3 min

If the sign-up form will not let you through, the message on the screen tells you why. Here is what each one means.

1. **"That username is already taken."** Someone else has it. Add a number or change a word.
2. **"An account with that email already exists."** You may already have signed up. Use [Log in](/login) or [reset your password](/forgot-password).
3. **"Password must contain at least one letter and one number."** Add a number or a letter and try again. Use at least 8 characters.
4. **"Please confirm you are 18 or older and accept the Terms of Service."** Tick the box above the button.
5. **"Please complete the security check and try again."** Wait for the small security check to finish, then tap the button again.
6. **Promo code messages.** "That promo code isn't valid or has ended" means the code is wrong or finished. "You have already used this promo code" means it can be used once per person.

Good to know:
- The site allows 3 sign-ups every 5 minutes from the same network. If you tried many times, wait a few minutes.
- Registration can be paused briefly during maintenance. A banner will say so.

=== sign-up-with-friend-link
category: Getting started
title: Signing up with a friend's invite link
excerpt: Use a friend's link so they earn a reward when you top up and play.
time: 2 min

If a friend shared a link with you, sign up through it. Your friend earns a reward when you add money for the first time, and you get the same {{welcome_bonus}} welcome bonus as everyone else.

=== install-raffleking-on-your-phone
category: Getting started
title: How to install RaffleKings on your phone's home screen
excerpt: Add the site as an app icon so it opens in one tap.
time: 2 min

You do not need an app store. You can add RaffleKings to your home screen so it opens like an app.

1. Open the site in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone).
2. Go to **Profile**, then **System**, then **Install App**. If your browser allows it, tap it and confirm.
3. If nothing pops up, open your browser menu. On Android it is the three dots. On iPhone it is the Share button.
4. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add**. The RaffleKings icon now appears with your other apps.

Good to know:
- If you do not see "Install App" in your Profile, the app is probably already installed.
- The installed app and the website show the same account and balance.

=== what-is-spending-wallet-and-winnings
category: Getting started
title: Spending Wallet vs Winnings: what is the difference?
excerpt: Two balances, two jobs. Know which money can be withdrawn.
time: 3 min
image: profile-wallet-cards
caption: On your Profile, "Spending Wallet" and "Winnings & Bonus" sit side by side.

You have two money balances. They look similar but they do different things.

1. **Spending Wallet.** This is the money you add by top-up, your welcome bonus, redeemed points and refunds. You use it to buy tickets. It cannot be withdrawn to a bank.
2. **Winnings & Bonus.** This is money you win, referral rewards and daily drop prizes. You can withdraw it to your bank, or move it to your Spending Wallet.

Good to know:
- Moving Winnings to the Spending Wallet is free and instant, but it only goes one way. You cannot move it back.
- You can pay for tickets from either balance at checkout.
- Your balances always show on the Profile page.

=== is-raffleking-safe
category: Getting started
title: Is RaffleKings safe? How your money and data are protected
excerpt: How payments, draws and your details are kept secure.
time: 3 min

You should always feel safe with your money. Here are the main protections.

1. **Payments** are made on Paystack's secure page. We never see or store your card number.
2. **Draws are checkable.** Every draw has a Verify page that proves it was random and followed the published rules.
3. **Your bank details** are encrypted, and your winnings can only be sent to accounts saved in your own profile.
4. **Play controls.** You can set spending limits or take a break on [Play Limits & Breaks](/support/tutorials/set-a-spending-limit).

Good to know:
- Never share your password. RaffleKings staff will never ask for it.
- Only trust messages from the official site and our support channels on the Help page.
