=== how-to-top-up-your-wallet
category: Deposits
title: How to top up your wallet
excerpt: Add money to your Spending Wallet by card, bank transfer or USSD.
time: 3 min
image: topup-form
caption: Type an amount or tap a quick amount, then tap "Pay securely".

Topping up puts money in your Spending Wallet so you can buy tickets. You pay on Paystack's secure page.

1. Open **Profile** and tap **Fund Wallet**, or tap the wallet balance at the top. This opens "Top Up Wallet".
2. Under **How much?** type an amount, or tap a quick amount such as ₦1,000 or ₦5,000. The smallest top-up is {{min_deposit}}.
3. Tap **Pay securely**.
4. You are taken to Paystack. Choose **card**, **bank transfer** or **USSD** and follow the prompts.
5. When you finish, you are brought back to the wallet page. A green message says "Payment confirmed" and the money is in your wallet.

Good to know:
- Money you top up is for tickets and cannot be withdrawn as cash. Only winnings can be withdrawn.
- Never type your card details anywhere except the Paystack page.

Stuck: See [Paid but not credited](/support/tutorials/paid-but-wallet-not-credited).

=== minimum-and-quick-top-up-amounts
category: Deposits
title: Smallest top-up and the quick amount buttons
excerpt: Which amounts you can add and what the buttons do.
time: 1 min

1. Open **Top Up Wallet**.
2. Look at **How much?**. It tells you the smallest amount, now {{min_deposit}}.
3. Tap a quick amount button, or type your own amount.

Good to know:
- The quick buttons only show amounts that are at least the minimum.
- If you type less than the minimum you will see "The smallest top-up is ..." and the button will not work.
- Staff can change the minimum, so always check the page.

=== pay-with-card-on-paystack
category: Deposits
title: Paying with your bank card
excerpt: Step by step on the Paystack page.
time: 3 min

1. Start a top-up and tap **Pay securely**.
2. On Paystack choose **Card**.
3. Type your card number, expiry date and CVV (the 3 digits at the back).
4. If your bank asks for a PIN or an OTP, type it. The OTP comes by SMS or from your banking app.
5. Wait for "Payment successful". You are returned to RaffleKings automatically.

Good to know:
- Do not close the page until you are returned to RaffleKings.
- Card payments are usually added in seconds.
- If your bank declines, call them, or try a bank transfer instead.

=== pay-with-bank-transfer-on-paystack
category: Deposits
title: Paying by bank transfer
excerpt: Send money to a one-time account number shown by Paystack.
time: 3 min

1. Start a top-up and tap **Pay securely**.
2. On Paystack choose **Bank Transfer**.
3. Paystack shows a bank name and a one-time account number, with an amount.
4. Open your banking app and send **exactly that amount** to that account.
5. Come back to the Paystack page and wait. It usually confirms within a minute or two.
6. You are returned to the wallet page when it is done.

Good to know:
- Use the account number only once. Each top-up gets a new one.
- Send the exact amount. A different amount can delay your credit.
- Transfers can be slower if your bank is busy.

=== pay-with-ussd-on-paystack
category: Deposits
title: Paying with USSD
excerpt: Pay from your bank's USSD code without internet banking.
time: 3 min

1. Start a top-up and tap **Pay securely**.
2. On Paystack choose **USSD**.
3. Choose your bank. Paystack shows a USSD code.
4. Dial the code on the phone number linked to your bank account.
5. Follow the prompts and confirm.
6. Wait on the Paystack page until it says the payment is done.

Good to know:
- USSD needs a phone network signal and the phone registered with your bank.
- Dial the code from the same phone line you registered with the bank.

=== paid-but-wallet-not-credited
category: Deposits
title: I paid but my wallet has not been credited
excerpt: What to check, how long to wait and how to get help fast.
time: 4 min
image: topup-howitworks
caption: The wallet page has a "Payment taken but not showing? Tell us" link at the bottom.

Most payments appear in seconds. If yours does not, follow these steps in order.

1. Wait 5 minutes and pull the page down to refresh. Open **Top Up Wallet** and look under **Recent top-ups**. "Waiting" means we are confirming it.
2. Check **Transaction History** for a "Wallet Deposit".
3. Look in your email for "Deposit confirmed". It is sent when the money arrives.
4. If it is still missing, tap **Payment taken but not showing? Tell us** at the bottom of the wallet page.
5. Choose Withdrawal / Deposit and include: the amount, the time, the bank you paid from and your Paystack receipt or bank alert screenshot.

Good to know:
- If the status says "Checking", our team is reviewing the payment. It will be credited or refunded.
- We never need your card number or PIN.

Stuck: The more detail you send, the faster we solve it. Send a screenshot of your bank alert.

=== payment-pending-or-checking
category: Deposits
title: What do "Waiting", "Checking" and "Not paid" mean on my top-up?
excerpt: Each top-up status in plain English.
time: 2 min

Under **Recent top-ups** each payment has a label.

1. **Paid**: money has been added to your wallet.
2. **Waiting**: Paystack has not confirmed the payment yet. Wait a few minutes and refresh.
3. **Checking**: your payment did not match the amount we asked for, so our team is checking it. Contact support if it takes long.
4. **Not paid**: the payment was not completed. Normally no money was taken. If your bank debited you, contact support.

Good to know:
- The page checks for you for about 30 seconds after you return, then stops. Refresh the page later if needed.

=== deposit-failed-money-deducted
category: Deposits
title: My bank deducted money but the top-up failed
excerpt: What to do when your bank alert and the site disagree.
time: 3 min

1. Do not pay again straight away.
2. Wait 10 minutes. Some banks take a little time to confirm.
3. Check **Transaction History** and your email for "Deposit confirmed".
4. If nothing shows, tap **Payment taken but not showing? Tell us** on the wallet page.
5. Send your bank alert (a screenshot), the amount, and the time.

Good to know:
- A payment that really failed is normally returned to your account by your bank within a few working days. Ask them for the reversal timeline.
- Paystack and the bank keep a record of every payment, so it can be traced.

=== deposit-error-could-not-start-payment
category: Deposits
title: "We couldn't start your payment right now"
excerpt: What this message means and what to try next.
time: 2 min

This message means the payment page could not open. No money has been taken.

1. Wait 2 to 3 minutes.
2. Check your internet connection.
3. Tap **Pay securely** again.
4. If it still fails, try again later or from a different network (switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data).

Good to know:
- We automatically use a backup payment partner if Paystack fails to start.
- If you see this repeatedly, [contact support](/support?new=1) and tell us the time.

=== top-up-while-on-a-break
category: Deposits
title: Why I cannot top up during a play break
excerpt: Topping up is switched off while a break is active.
time: 2 min

If you set a **break** from Play Limits & Breaks, you cannot top up, buy tickets or spin until it ends. You can still withdraw your winnings and contact support.

1. Open **Profile**, then **Play Limits & Breaks**.
2. Check the date your break ends.
3. After that date, top up as normal.

Good to know:
- A break cannot be ended early. This is deliberate so it helps you stay in control.
- See [Take a break](/support/tutorials/take-a-break-from-playing).

=== deposits-paused-message
category: Deposits
title: "This is paused for a short while": deposits are paused
excerpt: What the yellow notice on the wallet page means.
time: 1 min

Sometimes top-ups are switched off for a short time, for example during a payment-partner upgrade.

1. Read the notice at the top of the wallet page.
2. Try again after a short while.
3. Meanwhile, you can still use money already in your wallet.

Good to know:
- No money is taken while a feature is paused.

=== top-up-for-checkout-return
category: Deposits
title: Topping up in the middle of checkout
excerpt: Short of money while paying? Top up without losing your numbers.
time: 3 min
image: checkout

1. At checkout, if your balance is short, you see "Your wallet is ... short".
2. Tap **Top up** and the shortfall amount.
3. Pay on Paystack.
4. You come straight back to checkout. A message says "Top-up confirmed".
5. Tap **Pay** to finish.

Good to know:
- Your numbers are held for {{hold_minutes}} minutes. Be quick so they are not released.
- You can also cover a shortfall with your Winnings. See [Pay from winnings](/support/tutorials/pay-for-tickets-with-winnings).

=== move-winnings-to-your-wallet
category: Deposits
title: How to move winnings to your Spending Wallet
excerpt: Use your winnings to buy more tickets.
time: 2 min
image: move-winnings

1. Open **Profile**. Under **Winnings & Bonus** tap **Transfer**, or open Top Up Wallet and tap **Move to wallet**.
2. Type an amount or tap **All**.
3. Tap **Move to wallet**.
4. You will see "Done!" and the money is in your Spending Wallet.

Good to know:
- It is free and instant.
- It works one way only. Wallet money cannot be moved back to Winnings or withdrawn.
- You can also pay for tickets straight from Winnings at checkout.

=== wallet-balance-seems-wrong
category: Deposits
title: My balance looks wrong: how to check it
excerpt: Use Transaction History to follow each naira.
time: 3 min

1. Open **Profile**, then **Transaction History**.
2. Use the chips **Money In** and **Money Out** to separate entries.
3. Check recent items: Wallet Deposit, Ticket Purchase, Earnings to Wallet, Bank Withdrawal, Withdrawal Refunded, Referral Bonus, Raffle Win and Points Redeemed.
4. Remember there are two balances: Spending Wallet and Winnings.
5. If a number still does not add up, [message support](/support?new=1) with the date and amount.

Good to know:
- A withdrawal takes money from Winnings at once, so Winnings goes down as soon as you request.
- If it is declined, it comes back to Winnings.
