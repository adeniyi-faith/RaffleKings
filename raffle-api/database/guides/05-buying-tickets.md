=== how-to-buy-raffle-tickets
category: Buying tickets
title: How to buy tickets, from start to finish
excerpt: The full buying journey, from choosing a raffle to your receipt.
time: 4 min
image: raffle-page
caption: A raffle page shows the prize, the price, how many tickets are sold and "Get tickets".

1. Tap **Raffles** in the bottom bar and open a raffle.
2. Tap **Get tickets**.
3. Pick a bundle or your own quantity, then tap **Select numbers**.
4. Pick your numbers on the grid, or tap **Quick Pick**.
5. Tap **Checkout Now**. Your numbers are held for {{hold_minutes}} minutes.
6. Choose to pay from **Wallet** or **Winnings**, then tap **Pay**.
7. A "You're in!" box shows your ticket numbers. They also appear in **My Tickets**.

Good to know:
- You can look around and pick numbers without an account, but you need to log in to pay.
- If you do not have enough money, the page offers to top up and bring you back.

=== browse-and-filter-raffles
category: Buying tickets
title: Finding the right raffle: search, filters and sorting
excerpt: Use search, prize type and price to narrow the list.
time: 2 min
image: raffles-list

1. Open the **Raffles** tab.
2. Type in the **Search prizes** box.
3. Tap a chip: All, Cash, Gadgets, Vouchers or Other.
4. Use **Min price** and **Max price** to set a budget.
5. Use the sort menu: Newest, Closing soon, Price low to high, Price high to low.
6. Tap a raffle to open it.

Good to know:
- "Ending Soon" shows raffles that are more than half sold.
- Finished raffles stay on the list for {{list_closed_days}} days with the winners shown in the Hall of Fame.

=== read-a-raffle-page
category: Buying tickets
title: How to read a raffle page
excerpt: What each number and badge on a raffle means.
time: 3 min
image: raffle-page

1. **Prize name** and badges such as "Grand prize" or "Flash raffle".
2. **Price per ticket**, for example "₦500 a ticket".
3. **Progress bar**: how many tickets are sold and how many are left.
4. **Countdown**: how long until sales close.
5. **N viewing**: how many people are looking at the raffle.
6. **Prizes and your chances**: shows your odds for different ticket counts.
7. **How this draw works**: the rules locked in before the draw.
8. **Daily Drop** (on some raffles): money shared with ticket holders every day.

=== choose-how-many-tickets
category: Buying tickets
title: How many tickets should I buy? Understanding your chances
excerpt: See how more tickets raise your chance, then decide what fits your budget.
time: 3 min
image: ticket-picker

1. Open a raffle and tap **Get tickets**.
2. Look at the chance shown for each bundle.
3. Compare the price and the saving.
4. Pick the quantity that fits your budget. Never spend more than you can afford to lose.

Good to know:
- Each ticket is one chance. Ten tickets is ten chances in that raffle.
- Set a limit on [Play Limits](/support/tutorials/set-a-spending-limit) if you want help to stay in control.

=== pick-your-lucky-numbers
category: Buying tickets
title: How to pick your ticket numbers
excerpt: Tap the numbers you want on the grid.
time: 3 min
image: pick-numbers
caption: Available, yours, sold and held numbers each have their own colour.

1. After choosing your bundle, you reach the number grid.
2. Tap the numbers you want. You can pick as many as the tickets you chose.
3. A yellow number is yours. Tap it again to swap it.
4. Check the **Total Pay** at the bottom.
5. Tap **Checkout Now**.

Good to know:
- "SOLD" numbers are taken. "HELD" numbers are being held by another player.
- The grid updates live, so a number can change colour while you look.

=== cannot-pick-more-numbers

=== number-sold-or-held
category: Buying tickets
title: A number is "SOLD" or "HELD": what is the difference?
excerpt: Why some numbers cannot be picked.
time: 2 min

1. **SOLD** means someone already paid for it. It will not come back.
2. **HELD** means someone is in checkout with it right now. If they do not pay in time, the number is released.
3. Pick another number, or wait a few minutes and check again.

Good to know:
- A hold lasts {{hold_minutes}} minutes.
- Messages like "Number N is being held by another player right now" are about this.

=== number-hold-timer-explained
category: Buying tickets
title: The {{hold_minutes}}-minute number hold timer
excerpt: How long your numbers are kept and what happens at zero.
time: 3 min
image: checkout

When you tap **Checkout Now**, your numbers are held for you for {{hold_minutes}} minutes. You can see the countdown.

1. Finish paying before the clock reaches zero.
2. Refreshing the page does not restart the clock.
3. If the time runs out, a message says "Time's up". Tap **Get my numbers back** to try to get them again, if nobody else has taken them.

Good to know:
- You cannot hold more than {{max_held_numbers}} numbers at once.
- When you pay, the hold ends and the numbers are yours.

=== time-ran-out-numbers-released
category: Buying tickets
title: "Time's up. Your numbers were released": what now?
excerpt: Get your numbers back or choose new ones.
time: 2 min

1. Tap **Get my numbers back**. If they are still free, they are held again.
2. If someone took them, you will see "Some of your numbers were taken". Tap **Pick replacement numbers**.
3. Pay faster this time. Top up your wallet before you start if your balance is low.

=== numbers-taken-by-someone-else
category: Buying tickets
title: "Number N was just taken by someone else"
excerpt: Another player paid first. No money was taken from you.
time: 2 min

1. Read the message. It always says "Nothing was charged".
2. Tap **Pick other numbers**.
3. Choose a replacement and tap **Checkout Now** again.

Good to know:
- When many people play a hot raffle, popular numbers go quickly.
- You are never charged for a number you did not receive.

=== checkout-step-by-step
category: Buying tickets
title: Checkout step by step
excerpt: Every part of the Secure Checkout screen, explained.
time: 4 min
image: checkout
caption: Secure Checkout shows your numbers, your balances and the Pay button.

1. **Order Summary**: raffle, number of tickets, price and "Selected Numbers". Tap **Change** to edit numbers.
2. **Promo code**: if shown, tap "Have a promo code?" to add one.
3. **Payment**: choose Wallet or Winnings. The balance of each is shown.
4. **Timer**: the time left to pay while your numbers are held.
5. **Pay button**: tap it once. It says "Processing...".
6. A box says "You're in!" with your ticket numbers.

Good to know:
- Wait on the page after tapping Pay. Do not tap twice.
- A receipt is sent to your email.

=== not-enough-money-at-checkout
category: Buying tickets
title: "Your wallet is short": how to finish the purchase
excerpt: Three simple choices when your balance is too low.
time: 3 min

1. Read how much you are short.
2. Choose one:
   - **Use your winnings to cover it**, if you have Winnings.
   - **Pay from your wallet instead** after changing numbers or quantity.
   - **Top up** the shortfall on Paystack and return to checkout.
3. Pay before the timer ends.

=== checkout-error-price-changed
category: Buying tickets
title: "The ticket price has changed" or "Price mismatch"
excerpt: Review your order and pay again.
time: 2 min

1. Tap back to the raffle page.
2. Look at the current price per ticket.
3. Reselect your tickets and numbers.
4. Check out again.

Good to know:
- Price changes can happen when staff update a raffle, or when a discount such as the Golden Box offer ends.
- No money is taken when you see this message.

=== raffle-closed-at-checkout
category: Buying tickets
title: "This raffle has ended / sold out / closed"
excerpt: Why you cannot buy and what to do.
time: 2 min

1. Read the message. It says "No money has been taken".
2. Tap **Browse open raffles** and choose another.
3. If you already paid for tickets in it, find them under **My Tickets**.

Good to know:
- A raffle ends when all tickets are sold or when the closing date finishes.
- A cancelled raffle refunds everyone in full to the balance they paid from.

=== golden-box-offer-explained
category: Buying tickets
title: The Golden Box offer: what it is and how to claim it
excerpt: An extra discount on the order you left behind.
time: 3 min

If you leave checkout without paying, you may see a "Golden Offer" banner on the Raffles page.

1. Open **Raffles** and look for the gold banner.
2. It shows your order, the new price and a countdown ({{golden_offer_minutes}} minutes).
3. Tap **Claim**.
4. Pay within {{golden_claim_minutes}} minutes at the lower price.

Good to know:
- It gives {{golden_percent}}% off for the **same raffle and the same number of tickets**.
- Orders must be at least {{golden_minimum}} at normal price.
- After you use one, there is a {{golden_cooldown_days}}-day pause before the next.
- If it runs out, you will see "This offer has ended".

=== how-to-read-my-tickets
category: Buying tickets
title: How to read the My Tickets page
excerpt: What Active, Expired, Concluded and "You won!" mean.
time: 3 min
image: my-tickets

1. Open **Profile**, then **My Tickets**.
2. Each card is a raffle. It shows when you purchased, and your numbers.

=== bonus-entries-explained
category: Buying tickets
title: Free bonus entries: what they are
excerpt: Extra chances you earn without paying.
time: 3 min

Bonus entries are free extra chances in a draw. They show on My Tickets as "+N free bonus entries".

1. Earn them through loyalty tiers, the Season Pass, unlocking a link with friends or forming a team.
2. They join the draw like tickets in the same raffle.
3. If one wins, the ticket reads "Free bonus entry".

Good to know:
- Loyalty bonus entries only count in raffles that switch that rule on.
