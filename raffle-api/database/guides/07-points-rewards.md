=== what-are-points
category: Points & rewards
title: What are points and how do I earn them?
excerpt: Points are a separate reward currency you can turn into wallet money.
time: 3 min
image: rewards-daily

Points are not naira. They are rewards you earn for daily visits, quick tasks and more.

1. Open **My Rewards** in the bottom bar.
2. Collect your **daily reward**, finish **Quick Tasks**, play the **Daily Predictions** and level up the **Season Pass**.
3. See your points total at the top.
4. Redeem them for wallet money, or use them to spin.

Good to know:
- {{points_per_naira}} points are worth ₦1.
- Redeemed points go to your Spending Wallet and can be used for tickets.

=== missed-a-day-streak
category: Points & rewards
title: I missed a day and my streak reset
excerpt: How the 7-day streak works.
time: 2 min

1. Each day in a row pays more points.
2. If you miss a day, you start again from Day 1.
3. After Day 7 the cycle starts over.

Good to know:
- The day ends at midnight Nigerian time, so claim before then.

=== redeem-points-for-wallet-money
category: Points & rewards
title: How to turn points into wallet money
excerpt: Redeem all your points in one tap.
time: 2 min
image: rewards-redeem

1. Open **My Rewards**.
2. Find the card "Your points are worth ₦...".
3. Tap **Redeem Now**.
4. A message says "Redeemed!" and the money is in your Spending Wallet.

Good to know:
- {{points_per_naira}} points = ₦1. You need at least {{min_redeem_points}} points.
- It redeems all your points at once.
- Redeemed money buys tickets and cannot be withdrawn.

=== quick-tasks-how-they-work
category: Points & rewards
title: Quick Tasks: earn points for simple actions
excerpt: Tap Go, do the task, return and claim.
time: 3 min
image: rewards-tasks

1. Open **My Rewards** and scroll to **Quick Tasks**.
2. Tap **Go** on a task. It opens a link or asks for permission.
3. Do the task and come back.
4. Wait about {{task_wait_seconds}} seconds until the button says **Claim**, then tap it.
5. You see "Task Complete!"

Good to know:
- Enable Notifications gives {{task_notifications}} points, Join our Community {{task_community}}, Follow on WhatsApp {{task_whatsapp_follow}}, and Share on WhatsApp {{task_whatsapp_share}}.
- Each task can be done once, except WhatsApp sharing, which can be done once a day.

=== spin-and-win-how-it-works
category: Points & rewards
title: How to play Spin & Win
excerpt: Spin the wheel for points.
time: 3 min
image: spin

1. Open **My Rewards** and tap **Spin & Win**.
2. Tap **Spin**. It costs {{spin_cost}} points, unless you have a free spin.

- Prizes are points, not naira.
- The result is decided by the server before the wheel moves.

=== spin-not-working
category: Points & rewards
title: The spin did not go through or I cannot spin
excerpt: Fix the common spin problems.
time: 2 min

1. **"You need N more points to spin."** Earn more points first.
2. **"The spin did not go through. No points were taken."** Check your connection and try again.
3. **"Spin & Win is paused."** Try again later.
4. **Break message.** Spinning is off during a play break.

=== comeback-offers-explained
category: Points & rewards
title: Personal offers sent just for you
excerpt: How to claim a comeback offer.
time: 2 min

If these are on, you may get a personal offer by message, email or alert.

1. Open the offer from **Notifications**.
2. Read the prize and the time left.
3. Tap **Claim**.
4. Then tap **Pick my tickets** or **See my rewards**.

Good to know:
- Offers last a limited time.
- Ticket credit goes to your wallet and cannot be withdrawn.
