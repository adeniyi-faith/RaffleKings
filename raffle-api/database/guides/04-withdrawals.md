=== how-to-withdraw-your-winnings
category: Withdrawals
title: How to withdraw your winnings to your bank
excerpt: Request a withdrawal in four steps.
time: 3 min
image: withdraw-form
caption: The Withdraw Funds page shows your earnings, your bank and the amount.

You can withdraw money from your **Winnings** balance to your own Nigerian bank account.

1. Open **Profile** and tap **Withdraw** under Winnings & Bonus.
2. Check **Destination Account**. If none is shown, tap **Add Bank Account** first.
3. Type the amount, tap **MAX**, or tap a quick amount. The smallest withdrawal is {{min_withdrawal}}.
4. Tap **Withdraw Funds**.
5. A box says "Request Submitted". Your money is on its way.

Good to know:
- Only Winnings can be withdrawn. Money in your Spending Wallet cannot.
- The money leaves your Winnings balance as soon as you ask.
- You will be told by email and in Notifications when it is sent.

Stuck: Read [How long does a withdrawal take?](/support/tutorials/how-long-does-a-withdrawal-take)

=== how-long-does-a-withdrawal-take
category: Withdrawals
title: How long does a withdrawal take?
excerpt: What happens after you tap Withdraw and when to expect your money.
time: 3 min

After you request a withdrawal, our team checks it and sends it to your bank. The page says funds usually arrive within 24 hours.

1. Your request is saved and the amount leaves your Winnings balance.
2. Our team checks and sends the money. Smaller amounts may be sent automatically by Paystack.
3. When it is sent you get an email called "Withdrawal sent" and a message in Notifications.
4. Your bank then shows the credit. Most banks show it within minutes of the transfer.

Good to know:
- Larger withdrawals (above {{auto_payout_max}}) are paid by hand by our team, so they can take longer.
- You do not have to do anything while you wait. Do not request the same withdrawal again.
- Banks can be slow at night or on public holidays.

=== receiving-your-withdrawal-alert
category: Withdrawals
title: How you are told that your withdrawal was paid
excerpt: Where to look for the "Withdrawal sent" message.
time: 3 min
image: notifications
caption: The bell opens Notifications, where the "Withdrawal sent" message appears.

RaffleKings tells you about a paid withdrawal in two places, and your bank sends its own alert too.

1. **Email.** Look for "Withdrawal sent" in the email you signed up with. It says the amount that was sent to your bank.
2. **Notifications.** Tap the **bell icon**. A "Withdrawal sent" message shows with a "See history" button.
3. **Your bank alert.** Your bank sends its normal credit alert (SMS or app notification) when the money lands.

Good to know:
- We do not send withdrawal alerts by SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram. Be careful of anyone saying they are RaffleKings on those apps.
- The message shows the amount actually sent. If a verification fee applied, the amount can be a little lower than you asked for.
- A "Withdrawal declined" message means we returned the money to your Winnings, and gives a reason.

=== why-my-bank-has-not-credited-me
category: Withdrawals
title: We said "sent" but my bank has not shown the money
excerpt: What to check when the message says sent but your account has not changed.
time: 3 min

1. Check the amount in the "Withdrawal sent" message. It is the amount sent after any verification fee.
2. Check you picked the right bank account. Open **Bank Details** to see the number and name.
3. Wait 30 minutes and check your bank app again.
4. Check your bank statement, not only the SMS alert. Some banks delay alerts but update the balance.
5. If it is still missing, [open a ticket](/support?new=1) and send the date, amount and your account number.

Good to know:
- If you made a typing mistake in the number, the bank may return the money. Contact us as soon as possible.

=== withdrawal-pending-what-it-means
category: Withdrawals
title: My withdrawal is still pending: is that normal?
excerpt: What waiting means and when to contact support.
time: 3 min

A withdrawal is "pending" from the time you ask until our team sends it. Your Winnings has already been reduced during this time.

1. Check your **Notifications** and email for "Withdrawal sent" or "Withdrawal declined".
2. If you see nothing, wait up to 24 hours from your request.
3. If it has been over 24 hours, open a ticket from **Help** and choose "Withdrawal / Deposit".
4. Include the amount, the date and time of the request, and your bank account details.

Good to know:
- You will not see a "pending" label on Transaction History. The result comes by email and Notifications.
- Do not ask for a second withdrawal. It will not make the first one faster.

=== verification-required-before-withdrawal
category: Withdrawals
title: "Account Verification Required": what it means and your options
excerpt: The one-time rule for new accounts and how to complete it.
time: 4 min

If your total lifetime deposits are below {{verify_threshold}}, you must complete a one-time verification before your first withdrawal. This keeps accounts genuine.

1. Tap **Withdraw Funds**. A box says "Account Verification Required".
2. Choose one option:
   - **Fund {{verify_fee}} Now** takes you to top up. Once your top-ups total {{verify_threshold}}, you can withdraw.
   - **Pay {{verify_fee}} from Balance** takes the fee from your Winnings after you confirm.
3. The fee is **credited to your Spending Wallet** as ticket money. It is not lost.
4. After this one time, later withdrawals do not carry this fee.

Good to know:
- The box tells you the exact amount before anything is taken.
- If the fee comes from the withdrawal itself, the amount sent is lower by the fee.
- See [Verification fee maths](/support/tutorials/verification-fee-examples).

=== verification-fee-examples
category: Withdrawals
title: Verification fee examples: what you receive
excerpt: Simple numbers showing how the one-time fee works.
time: 3 min

The numbers below use a fee of {{verify_fee}}. Your site settings may differ.

1. **You have enough for both.** You want {{min_withdrawal}} and have more than {{min_withdrawal}} plus {{verify_fee}} in Winnings. We send the full amount and take the fee on top.
2. **You have just enough for the withdrawal.** The fee is taken from the withdrawal, so we send the amount minus {{verify_fee}}.
3. **You do not have enough.** The page tells you "Insufficient balance".
4. In every case the fee is credited to your Spending Wallet.

Good to know:
- Because the fee goes to your Spending Wallet, it counts as a deposit. After that, later withdrawals do not carry the fee.
- If a withdrawal is declined, your Winnings is returned.

=== insufficient-balance-on-withdrawal
category: Withdrawals
title: "Insufficient balance" when I try to withdraw
excerpt: Why the site says you are short and how to fix it.
time: 3 min

1. Check **Withdrawable Earnings** on the Withdraw page. It shows only your Winnings.
2. Remember that your **Spending Wallet** cannot be withdrawn.
3. If a verification fee applies, your Winnings must cover the amount plus the fee. Or the amount minus the fee must still be more than zero.
4. Try a smaller amount, or tap **MAX**.
5. Wait if you just made a withdrawal. The money leaves your Winnings immediately.

Good to know:
- A prize is added to Winnings only after staff credit it. Check My Tickets for "Paid into your winnings".

=== add-a-bank-account
category: Withdrawals
title: How to add a bank account
excerpt: Save the account your winnings should be paid into.
time: 3 min
image: bank-add-sheet
caption: Add the bank name, your 10-digit account number and the account name, then tap Save Account.

1. Open **Profile**, then **Bank Details**. Or tap **Add Bank Account** on the Withdraw page.
2. Tap **Link New Account**.
3. Search for and choose your bank (for example GTBank, OPay or Kuda).
4. Type your 10-digit account number.
5. Your bank confirms the account name. Check that it is yours.
6. Tap **Save Account**.

Good to know:
- You can save up to {{max_bank_accounts}} accounts.
- The account must be a Nigerian bank account in your own name.
- Only accounts with a bank-confirmed name qualify for automatic payout.
- If name checking is not switched on, you type the bank name, number and name yourself.

=== bank-account-errors
category: Withdrawals
title: Bank account errors explained
excerpt: Fix each message on the bank account form.
time: 3 min

1. **"Nigerian account numbers must be exactly 10 digits."** Check your number and type it again.
2. **"... has no account ..."** The bank and number do not match. Check both.
3. **"Choose your bank from the list."** Tap a bank in the list rather than typing a new name.
4. **"This account is already saved."** It is already in your list.
5. **"Maximum of 2 bank accounts allowed."** Remove one before adding another.
6. **"Enter the account name as it appears at the bank."** Use letters only, 3 to 80 characters.
7. **"We can't check bank accounts right now."** Wait a few minutes and try again.

=== wrong-account-name-withdrawal
category: Withdrawals
title: I saved the wrong bank details: what do I do?
excerpt: Act fast to protect your winnings.
time: 3 min

1. If you have **not** requested a withdrawal, remove the account and add the correct one.
2. If you **have** requested one that is still pending, [contact support](/support?new=1) immediately, with the correct details.

Good to know:
- Sending to the wrong account may not be reversible, so always check the name your bank shows before you save.

=== withdrawal-declined-what-next
category: Withdrawals
title: My withdrawal was declined: what next?
excerpt: Why a withdrawal can be declined and how your money comes back.
time: 3 min

If a withdrawal is declined, you get an email and a Notification called "Withdrawal declined" with a reason. The money is returned to your Winnings.

1. Read the **reason** in the message.
2. Check **Transaction History** for "Withdrawal Refunded".
3. Fix what the reason says, for example your bank details, then request again.
4. If you do not understand the reason, tap **Ask support** on the message.

Good to know:
- Reasons are written by our team for your case, so they are specific.
- Any verification fee taken in the declined request is returned to your Winnings.

=== why-spending-wallet-cannot-be-withdrawn
category: Withdrawals
title: Why I cannot withdraw my Spending Wallet
excerpt: Money for tickets stays for tickets.
time: 2 min

Money in your Spending Wallet came from a top-up, the welcome bonus, redeemed points or similar. It is meant for tickets. Only prize money, referral rewards and daily drop prizes are in Winnings and can be withdrawn.

1. Check which balance holds your money on **Profile**.
2. Use Spending Wallet money to buy tickets.
3. Any win goes to Winnings, and you can withdraw that.
