=== invite-friends-and-earn
category: Referrals & community
title: How to invite friends and earn
excerpt: Share your link and earn a share of their first top-up.
time: 3 min
image: referrals
caption: Your invite link and code are at the top of the Invite Friends page.

1. Open **My Rewards** and tap **Invite Friends**.
2. Tap **Copy** or **Share on WhatsApp**.
3. Your friend signs up with your link.
4. When they top up for the first time, you earn {{referral_commission}} of that first top-up in your Winnings.
5. When they buy their first ticket, they count as "playing" toward your ladder rewards.

Good to know:
- The commission is paid once per friend, on their first top-up.
- Your invite link works only if they sign up through it.
- Referral money is in Winnings, so it can be withdrawn.

=== referral-reward-not-received
category: Referrals & community
title: My friend joined but I got no reward
excerpt: Common reasons and what to check.
time: 3 min

1. Open **Invite Friends** and check whether your friend appears in the list.
2. If they are not there, they may have signed up without your link. A link must be used before the account is created.
3. If they appear as "Not played yet", the reward needs their first top-up.
4. Look for "Referral Bonus" in **Transaction History**.
5. Still missing? [Contact support](/support?new=1) with your friend's username.

Good to know:
- Fake or duplicate accounts get no rewards.

=== referral-ladder-rewards
category: Referrals & community
title: The referral ladder: rewards for friends who play
excerpt: Bonus points, spins and badges at 1, 5, 10 and 25 friends.
time: 3 min

1. Open **Invite Friends**.
2. A friend counts after they join with your link and buy a ticket.
3. At 1 friend you get {{ladder_first_points}} points and a badge.
4. Later steps (5, 10 and 25 friends) give more points, free spins and badges.

=== share-your-win-story
category: Referrals & community
title: How to share your win story
excerpt: Post a photo and earn thank-you points.
time: 3 min

1. Open **Winners**, then **Winner Stories**.
2. Tap **Share your win**.
3. Choose which win, add a photo or short video and an optional caption.
4. Tap **Post my story**.
5. After we approve it, you get {{story_points}} points.

Good to know:
- Do not show bank details or your address.
- Photos can be up to 5 MB and videos up to 20 MB.

=== unlock-a-free-entry
category: Referrals & community
title: Unlock a free bonus entry with friends
excerpt: Friends tap your link and you get a free entry.
time: 3 min

1. Buy at least one ticket in a raffle.
2. On the raffle page open **Boost and share**.
3. Tap **Get my unlock link** and share it.
4. When {{unlock_taps}} friends tap it, you get a free bonus entry.

Good to know:
- Friends need their own phones. They earn 20 points for tapping.

=== team-up-for-free-entries
category: Referrals & community
title: Team Up: form a team for free entries
excerpt: Fill a team within the time and everyone gains a free entry.
time: 3 min

1. Buy a ticket in the raffle.
2. Open **Boost and share** and tap **Start a team**.

=== red-envelopes-in-live-draw
category: Referrals & community
title: Red envelopes: send points to people in a live draw
excerpt: Share points with other viewers.
time: 3 min

1. In a live draw's chat tap **Send a red envelope**.
2. Choose points ({{envelope_min}} to {{envelope_max}}), how many people and an optional message.
3. Tap **Send**.
4. Others tap **Tap to open!** to get a share.

Good to know:
- Unopened points are returned after 10 minutes.
- You cannot open your own envelope.

=== public-player-cards-privacy
category: Referrals & community
title: Who can see my profile and wins?
excerpt: What other players see about you.
time: 2 min

1. Open **My Badges** from Profile.
2. Choose **Everyone can see it** or **Only me**.
3. Tick or untick "Show my wins on my profile".

Good to know:
- A public card shows only your username, picture and badges. Never your money, name, phone or email.

=== affiliate-dashboard-explained
category: Referrals & community
title: The Affiliate dashboard (for approved affiliates)
excerpt: Where affiliates see clicks, sign-ups and earnings.
time: 3 min

If you were approved as an affiliate, you will see an **Affiliate** link on Profile.

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Copy your link and share it.
3. Check Clicks, Sign-ups, Played and Topped up.
4. Earnings are paid into your Winnings after a short hold.

Good to know:
- Staff set your commission and how long it applies.
- If you are not an affiliate, this does not apply to you.
