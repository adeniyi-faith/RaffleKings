=== watch-a-live-draw
category: Draws & winning
title: How to watch a live draw
excerpt: See winners revealed one by one and chat while you watch.
time: 3 min
image: live-draw-card
caption: Tap "Watch The Live Draw" on the Winners tab.

1. Tap **Winners**, then **Watch The Live Draw**. Or open **Profile**, **Community**, **Live Draws**.
2. Under **Live now** tap **Watch now**. Under **Coming up** tap **Open** to wait for the start.
3. Winners are shown one at a time with their ticket numbers.
4. Log in to chat and react.

Good to know:
- Not every raffle has a live show. Others are drawn straight away and the winners go to the Hall of Fame.
- Chat messages cannot contain links or phone numbers.

=== verify-a-draw-step-by-step
category: Draws & winning
title: How to verify a draw yourself
excerpt: Check that a draw was random and followed the rules.
time: 4 min
image: verify
caption: The Verify page shows four checks. All should be marked as passed.

1. Open **Profile**, **My Tickets**, and tap **Verify draw** on the raffle. Or tap **Verify** on a winner in the Hall of Fame.
2. Read the **commitment** shown. It is a fingerprint of a secret code, published before the draw.
3. After the draw, the secret code is shown too.
4. Look at the four checks: the code matches the fingerprint, the ticket list matches, the rules were followed and running it again gives the same winners.
5. All four should be marked as passed.

Good to know:
- Before a draw runs you will see "This draw hasn't run yet".
- Nobody, including staff, can change the result after the fingerprint is published.

=== i-won-what-happens-next
category: Draws & winning
title: I won! What happens next?
excerpt: How your prize reaches you.
time: 3 min

1. You get an email "You won!", a message in Notifications and a phone alert if you allowed them.
2. Open **My Tickets**. Your win shows "You won ₦X!" with the ticket.
3. For **cash**, it says "Being paid into your winnings" until our team credits it, then "Paid into your winnings".
4. For a **physical prize**, it says "We'll contact you about delivery".
5. Your cash appears in **Winnings & Bonus** on Profile.

Good to know:
- Prizes are checked before they are credited, so it is not always instant.
- You can then [withdraw](/support/tutorials/how-to-withdraw-your-winnings) your winnings or use them to buy more tickets.

=== prize-not-yet-credited
category: Draws & winning
title: I won but the prize has not shown in my balance
excerpt: What "Being paid into your winnings" means.
time: 3 min

1. Open **My Tickets** and find the win.
2. If it says "Being paid into your winnings", our team is still reviewing and crediting it.
3. Check your **Notifications** and email for updates.
4. If it has been more than 24 hours, [open a ticket](/support?new=1) and choose "Claiming a Prize".

Good to know:
- A win shows in **Transaction History** as "Raffle Win" once credited.

=== claim-a-physical-prize
category: Draws & winning
title: How to claim a gadget or voucher prize
excerpt: Prizes that are not cash.
time: 3 min

1. Open **My Tickets** and look at your winning card.
2. It will say "We'll contact you about delivery".
3. Keep your phone and email available. We will contact you.
4. Check **Notifications** for a message from us.
5. If you hear nothing, open a ticket and choose "Claiming a Prize".

Good to know:
- Never pay anyone to "release" a prize. We never ask for payment to give you a prize.

=== why-i-did-not-win
category: Draws & winning
title: I did not win. Was the draw fair?
excerpt: How to check, and what to do next.
time: 3 min

1. Open **Verify draw** from My Tickets.
2. Check that all four checks passed.
3. Look at the winning ticket numbers in the Hall of Fame.
4. If you want, buy again in another raffle. Every raffle is a new draw.

Good to know:
- Having more tickets raises your chance, but never guarantees a win.
- Past losses do not make a future win more or less likely.

=== one-prize-per-person-rule
category: Draws & winning
title: Why can a person win only once in a raffle?
excerpt: The one-win and recent-winner rules.
time: 2 min

1. By default one person can win one prize in a raffle.
2. People who won any raffle in the last {{recent_winner_days}} days sit the next draw out, so more people get a chance.
3. The raffle's "How this draw works" panel lists the exact rules.

Good to know:
- Your tickets are not lost. Check the rules on that raffle page before you buy.

=== daily-drop-explained
category: Draws & winning
title: What is a Daily Drop?
excerpt: Extra prize money shared every day on some raffles.
time: 3 min

1. Some raffles show a **Daily Drop** row on the raffle page.
2. Every day at a set time, a share of new ticket sales is split between random ticket holders.
3. Winners get the money straight into Winnings and a notification.
4. Your ticket still stays in the main draw.

Good to know:
- You can win one share per day.
- There is a "Check this drop" link so you can verify it.

=== loyalty-tiers-explained
category: Draws & winning
title: Loyalty tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond
excerpt: Play weekly to climb and earn free bonus entries.
time: 3 min

Your tier depends on your play in the last {{loyalty_weeks}} weeks.

1. **Bronze** is where everybody starts.
2. **Silver:** 3 active weeks and 10 tickets. Gives +1 bonus entry.
3. **Gold:** 5 active weeks and 30 tickets. Gives +2.
4. **Diamond:** 7 active weeks and 80 tickets. Gives +3.
5. See your progress on the **Rewards** page.

Good to know:
- A week counts when you buy at least one ticket.
- Bonus entries apply only in raffles that switch the loyalty rule on.

=== lucky-meter
category: Draws & winning
title: The Lucky Meter: ticket credit for near misses
excerpt: How the meter fills and what it pays.
time: 2 min

If the Lucky Meter is on, money you spent on tickets in raffles you did not win fills a meter. Each time it reaches its target, a ticket credit is added to your wallet.

1. Play as normal.
2. After draws finish, the meter updates.
3. When it is full, you get a notification and the credit.

Good to know:
- Free bonus entries and cancelled raffles do not count.
- It may not be switched on yet. Watch for the card on Rewards.
