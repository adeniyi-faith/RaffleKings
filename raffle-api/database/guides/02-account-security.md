=== reset-a-forgotten-password
category: Account & security
title: How to reset a forgotten password
excerpt: Get a 6-digit code by email and choose a new password.
time: 3 min
image: forgot-password
caption: Type your email and tap "Send Code".

If you cannot remember your password, you can set a new one using a code we email you.

1. On the login page tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Type your account email and tap **Send Code**.
3. Open your email and find "Your RaffleKings password reset code". Copy the 6-digit number.
4. On the next page type the code and tap **Verify Code**.
5. Type a new password (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number) and tap **Update Password**.
6. Log in with your new password.

Good to know:
- The code works for {{reset_code_minutes}} minutes.
- You get 5 wrong tries per code. After that, ask for a new one.
- For your safety we always say "If that email has an account, a code has been sent", whether or not the email exists.
- Changing your password signs you out of other phones.

Stuck: See [The reset code does not arrive](/support/tutorials/reset-code-not-arriving).

=== reset-code-not-arriving
category: Account & security
title: The password reset code has not arrived
excerpt: Where to look and what to try when the email is missing.
time: 3 min

1. Wait two minutes. Emails can be slow.
2. Check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder, and the Promotions tab in Gmail.
3. Make sure you typed the same email you signed up with. A small typo means no code.
4. On the code page tap **Resend code**. A new code replaces the old one.
5. Add {{support_email}} to your contacts, then try again.

Good to know:
- You can ask for 3 codes every 5 minutes. If you reach the limit, wait and try again.
- Only the newest code works. Older codes stop working when you ask for a new one.
- If you cannot remember which email you used, try the other emails you own.

Stuck: Contact us through [Telegram support]({{telegram_support}}) or email {{support_email}} from the email you signed up with.

=== reset-code-invalid-or-expired
category: Account & security
title: "That code is invalid" or "expired": what to do
excerpt: Fix reset code errors in a few steps.
time: 2 min

1. **"That code is invalid."** Check each digit. Copy and paste the code if you can.
2. **"That code has expired. Request a new one."** Codes last {{reset_code_minutes}} minutes. Tap **Resend code**.
3. **"Too many wrong codes. Request a new one."** After 5 wrong tries the code is locked. Tap **Resend code** and use only the newest email.
4. Type the new code, tap **Verify Code**, and then set your new password.

Good to know:
- If you asked for several codes, the last one is the only one that works.
- Do not share the code with anyone, even someone who says they work for RaffleKings.

=== change-your-password
category: Account & security
title: How to change your password while logged in
excerpt: Pick a new password from your profile settings.
time: 2 min
image: edit-profile-security
caption: The Security section is at the bottom of Edit Profile.

1. Open **Profile**, then **Personal Details**.
2. Scroll to **Security**.
3. Type your new password in **New Password**. Use at least 8 characters with a letter and a number.
4. Type your old password in **Current Password**. This box appears when you change your password.
5. Tap **Save Changes**. You will see "Profile updated successfully!"

Good to know:
- You stay signed in on this phone. Your other devices are signed out.
- "Your current password is not right" means the old password is wrong. Use [Forgot password](/support/tutorials/reset-a-forgotten-password) if needed.

=== cannot-log-in
category: Account & security
title: I cannot log in: step-by-step fixes
excerpt: Work through the usual reasons a login fails.
time: 3 min
image: login-form

1. **Use your email, not your username.** The login box is for the email address you signed up with.
2. **Check your password.** Tap the eye icon to see it. Check CAPS LOCK.
3. **Wait if you tried many times.** You can try 5 times a minute. Wait one minute and try again.
4. **Reset the password** with [Forgot password](/support/tutorials/reset-a-forgotten-password).
5. **Check for a message.** "This account has been suspended" means staff have limited the account. Contact support.
6. **Try another browser** or update your browser if the page does not respond.

Good to know:
- "Incorrect username or password" is the same message for a wrong email and a wrong password, to protect your account.

Stuck: Send us a message on Telegram or email {{support_email}} and tell us the email you use.

=== account-suspended-what-to-do
category: Account & security
title: "This account has been suspended": what to do
excerpt: Why accounts get limited and how to ask for a review.
time: 2 min

A suspended or banned account cannot log in or withdraw. This usually follows a check by our team, for example if the rules were broken or an account looked duplicated.

1. Do not create a new account. This can make things worse.
2. Contact us on [Telegram]({{telegram_support}}) or at {{support_email}}.
3. Tell us your username and email, and say what you were doing.
4. Wait for our reply. We will explain the reason and what happens next.

Good to know:
- Some limits end on their own after a set date.
- Your money is safe while we review.

=== keep-your-account-secure
category: Account & security
title: 8 simple habits to keep your account secure
excerpt: Stay safe from scams and from people trying to get in.
time: 3 min

1. Use a password that nobody can guess, and not the one you use elsewhere.
2. Never share your password, reset code or bank details. Staff will never ask for them.
3. Only use the official site. Check the address before you log in.
4. Log out on shared phones.
5. Keep your email account secure, because reset codes go there.
6. Do not click prizes or "claim" links sent by strangers.
7. Pay only on the Paystack page that opens from the Top Up Wallet page.
8. Check your Notifications and Transactions for anything you do not recognise, and contact us right away.

=== why-two-accounts-are-not-allowed
category: Account & security
title: One person, one account: why duplicates are not allowed
excerpt: What happens when someone opens more than one account.
time: 2 min

Each person may have one account. This keeps every draw and every reward fair.

1. If you have forgotten your login, [reset your password](/support/tutorials/reset-a-forgotten-password) instead of opening a new account.
2. If you lost access to your email, contact support. We can help you recover the account.
3. Do not register using a friend's or relative's details just to get bonuses.

Good to know:
- Referral rewards for fake or duplicate accounts are removed.
- Friends in the same home can each have their own account if each is a different person.

=== what-the-verified-tag-means
category: Account & security
title: Do I need to verify my email or phone?
excerpt: There is no email or phone verification step on RaffleKings.
time: 1 min

You do not need to verify your email or phone number to play. You will see a "Verified" tag on your Profile. It is just a label and not a step you must complete.

1. Make sure your email is spelled correctly in **Personal Details**.
2. Keep your phone number up to date, in case support needs it.
3. For withdrawals, add a bank account and check the name matches yours. See [Add a bank account](/support/tutorials/add-a-bank-account).

Good to know:
- For your first withdrawal you may be asked to complete a lifetime deposit of {{verify_threshold}}. See [Verification fee](/support/tutorials/verification-required-before-withdrawal).
