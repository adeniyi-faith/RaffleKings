=== set-a-spending-limit
category: Help & troubleshooting
title: How to set a daily, weekly or monthly spending limit
excerpt: Stay in control of how much you spend on tickets.
time: 3 min
image: play-limits
caption: Daily, weekly and monthly limits are set on Play Limits & Breaks.

1. Open **Profile**, then **Play Limits & Breaks**.
2. Under **Spending limits** type a Daily, Weekly or Monthly limit. The smallest is ₦100.
3. Tap **Save limits**.

Good to know:
- Lowering a limit works straight away.
- Raising or removing a limit starts after 24 hours, to give you time to think.
- Leave a box empty for no limit.
- Limits follow Nigerian time.

=== take-a-break-from-playing
category: Help & troubleshooting
title: How to take a break from playing
excerpt: Pause yourself for a day, a week, a month or longer.
time: 3 min

1. Open **Profile**, then **Play Limits & Breaks**.
2. Under **Take a break** choose 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, 6 months or 1 year.
3. Tap **Yes, start my break**.

Good to know:
- A break cannot be ended early.
- During a break you cannot buy tickets, top up or spin. You can still withdraw and contact support.

=== limit-reached-at-checkout

=== contact-support-open-a-ticket
category: Help & troubleshooting
title: How to contact support and open a ticket
excerpt: Reach our team in a few taps.
time: 3 min
image: support-form
caption: The "Open New Ticket" form.

1. Open **Profile**, then **Help**, then **Submit a support ticket**. Or go to [Help & Support](/support?new=1).
2. Choose an **Issue Type**.
3. Describe the problem in detail.
4. Add up to 3 screenshots, if helpful.
5. Tap **Submit Ticket**.

Good to know:
- Replies appear in the same place and are sent to your email and Notifications.
- You can also reach us on Telegram or at {{support_email}}.

=== write-a-good-support-message
category: Help & troubleshooting
title: What to include so support can help you faster
excerpt: A short checklist for a quick resolution.
time: 2 min

1. Say what you were doing and what you expected.
2. Include the date, time and amount.
3. Add the raffle name, if relevant.
4. Attach a screenshot of the message you saw.
5. Never include your password or card details.

=== upload-screenshots-to-support
category: Help & troubleshooting
title: How to attach screenshots to a ticket
excerpt: Add pictures of the problem.
time: 2 min

1. When you open a new ticket, tap the screenshot button.

If a picture is too big, take a new screenshot or crop it.

=== check-support-reply
category: Help & troubleshooting
title: How to see support's reply
excerpt: Where answers appear.
time: 2 min

1. Tap the **bell** for "Support replied".
2. Or open **Profile**, then **My support tickets**.
3. Tap your conversation. Reply in the box and tap **Send**.

Good to know:
- "open" means we are yet to reply. "pending" means we replied and wait for you. "resolved" means we think it is solved.
- You can reopen a ticket by replying.

=== limitations-of-the-site
category: Help & troubleshooting
title: What RaffleKings does not do: known limits
excerpt: Plain list of things you cannot do on the site.
time: 3 min

It helps to know the limits so there are no surprises.

1. Spending Wallet money cannot be withdrawn.
2. Winnings can be moved to the wallet, but wallet money cannot be moved back.
3. A play break cannot be ended early.
4. You cannot change your username.
5. Your birthday can be set only once.
6. You can save up to {{max_bank_accounts}} bank accounts, all Nigerian.
7. Payments are made on the Paystack page, never inside RaffleKings.
8. We do not send withdrawal alerts by SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram.
9. Account deletion is not available inside the app. Ask support.
